# W04: islands rise and sink

Features: W04, W01, W05, A05

The user picked W04 next after v1's follow-ups, then W06 bridges
(2026-10-07). Islands used to appear and vanish with their sessions, and
whoever stood on an ended session's island was put back on the platform.

## Decisions (user's, 2026-10-07)

- **Arriving at a new session's island:** it rises first, then you're
  taken there. Rejected: a viewpoint offshore to watch it rise (it needed
  somewhere to stand in open water), and riding it up (the camera would
  start at or under the opaque water).
- **Sessions already running when the world connects:** no rise for now.
  I recommended they all rise together as an intro; the user deferred it
  to attaching to running sessions (open question 5), since that's when
  the game will meet sessions it didn't start.
- **Ending:** I offered sinking under the player (the sea rule brings
  them back) or the old respawn. The user chose neither: the orchestrator
  and its agents go at once, and the empty island stays while you're on
  it, sinking only once you've left. Its bridges are to count as "on it"
  once W06 exists.
- **Motion only:** no splash, foam or sound yet.

## Built

`SessionIslands` tweens the island's y between -8 m and 0 (3 s ease-out
up, 4 s ease-in down; my picks). -8 m clears the dunes (about 3.2 m) and
the waves; the opaque sea hides the rest. The world's `teleport` waits for
`island_risen`. An ended session's island goes into a "left" list, checked
each frame against the player's position (the same reach as `session_at`,
twice the shoreline radius), and sinks once the player isn't over it.

The island's cluster spot had to outlive its session, or a new session
could rise into the spot of an island still standing there.
`SessionClusters.hold` swaps the session for a hold key in the cluster's
members: it keeps the island index and the cluster's slot, but isn't
listed by `sessions` or `ids`, so the menu doesn't show a cluster with
only an empty island. The repository's next session still joins that
cluster, beside the empty island. `remove(hold)` frees it when the island
is freed. Hold keys are `hold:N`; session ids are UUIDs, so they can't
clash.

## Finding a new island

After trying Part A of the live check, the user couldn't tell where the
new island was rising: nothing pointed to it. Since there is to be an
option to turn off being taken to a new island (deferred ideas), the game
has to show where a new session went. Offered: a beacon plus an edge
arrow (my recommendation), a beacon only, a line of text with the
direction, or waiting for W06's minimap. The user chose the beacon and
arrow, built with W04 rather than later, and asked that it be marked a
placeholder, as all UI is.

The beacon is A05's `AttentionBeacon` with a settable colour, green
(orange means waiting), from the island's middle so it rises with it.
`NewIslandPointers` draws one arrow per unvisited island when it's behind
the camera or off the inset screen, with the project and distance. Being
on the island (by teleport too) clears both. In the headless tests the
window is 64 × 64 px, smaller than the 56 px margin's two sides, which
gave a negative rectangle; the margin is now at most a quarter of the
window's smaller side.

## Tests

- `clusters_test`: a hold takes the session out and hides an emptied
  cluster but keeps its spot; the repository's next session joins beside
  it; releasing frees cluster and spot.
- `world_test` (by hand): a snapshot's island isn't rising; ending a
  session takes its orchestrator and closes the chat, and the island stays
  while you're on it; a `session.created` island starts under water and
  rises; teleporting waits for it; leaving the empty island sinks and
  frees it, and its spot is free again; a session ending mid-rise sinks
  from there; the beacon over a new island and none over a snapshot's;
  the arrow absent while you face the island and on the screen's edge,
  with "demo · N m", when it's behind you; both cleared once you've been
  there.
- `world_test --sidecar-dev-fake`: a session started from the menu rises
  before taking you there; ending it keeps the island until you leave,
  then it sinks; a resumed one rises the same way.

**Live check passed (2026-10-07)** with the user, with the fake and the
real `claude`: every step of the checklist (rise, beacon and arrow,
ending under you and sinking once you've left, sinking with you away).

A test snag: `_until` lambdas that captured the orchestrator or island
raised "Lambda capture at index 0 was freed" once they were freed, so
those waits hold a `WeakRef` instead.
