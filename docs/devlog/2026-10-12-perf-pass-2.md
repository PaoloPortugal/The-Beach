# The second performance pass: the Mobile renderer

Features: Architecture → Performance, Architecture → Rendering, C10, W09

The user asked for another optimization pass after C10's live check:
their machine (integrated AMD Radeon) couldn't run MSAA fast enough
under Forward+.

## Measuring (2026-10-12)

The layout demo (64 sessions) at 1920x1080, vsync off, noon, clear,
`seed(1234)` so every run builds the same islands; GPU time per frame,
median of 180 frames, at four views: the platform looking out and back,
an island (`arrival_point("d01")`), a bridge's middle above its deck
looking along it. Runs vary by 1-3 ms. (The first rounds' bridge view
was under the water, inside the bridge's box with its posts; fixed.)

Forward+, saving per frame by hiding or changing one thing:
- MSAA 2x +5-8 ms, 4x +10-15 ms; FXAA +1.5-2 ms, SMAA +3.5-4 ms.
- Sun shadows off -4 to -9 ms; Low shadows -2 to -5 ms; hard shadow
  filtering, a 60 m shadow distance: within the spread.
- Render scale 75 %: about a third less. Fill-bound, not triangles.
- The ocean -3 to -7 ms; the island ground -4 to -12 ms (the bridge
  view, where cliffs overlap); props, clouds, the seabed, bridges, the
  agents: about 1 ms or less each. Night (lamps, torches): +0-3 ms.
- Per-vertex shading on the islands' 1,143 materials: no gain, slower
  on the island view.
- **Depth pre-pass back on** (Watch items): -3 to -17 ms, but the black
  striped triangles are still there in 4.7.2, the latest release. Not
  an option.

**Mobile renderer**, the same scenes (ms, Forward+ → Mobile → Mobile
with MSAA 2x): platform out 18.5 → 10.1 → 14.4; back 21.1 → 12.7 →
15.9; island 15.6 → 10.3 → 14.3; bridge 31.5 → 16.0 → 21.3. Day,
night and storm screenshots look the same, apart from a slight
banding in Mobile's night sky (about 3 levels near the top, a faint
purple arc), still there with `use_debanding` on.

## Decision (user's, 2026-10-12)

**Switch to the Mobile renderer** (my recommendation; rejected: a
Forward+/Mobile choice in the settings, which needs a restart and
reverses C10's "no renderer setting"; staying on Forward+, where nothing
smaller measured beyond the spread). The Compatibility fallback stays.
Given up: SSAO, SSR, SDFGI and volumetric fog (none used), 8 lights per
mesh at most. `project.godot`: `renderer/rendering_method="mobile"`,
`anti_aliasing/quality/use_debanding=true` (its cost was within the
spread).

## The night sky's bands

Mobile keeps the 3D picture in 10 bits a channel over twice the usual
range (so values up to 2 fit), and the night sky's colours are very
dark in linear light, where that is a step every 0.002 or so: the
gradient fell into a few flat bands, each channel changing at its own
row, hence the purple arc. Godot's debanding works on the finished
picture, after that rounding, so it couldn't help; the sky's process
mode and radiance size only change the reflection map, not the sky you
see. Forward+ keeps 16-bit floats, so it had none.

Fix: the sky shader adds a dither of plus or minus half a step, noise
from the pixel's position (interleaved gradient noise), only under
Mobile (`CURRENT_RENDERER`) and not when drawing the reflection map
(`AT_CUBEMAP_PASS`). Screenshots with the contrast stretched six times
showed the bands gone and no visible noise at normal contrast; the
average colour at the top moved closer to Forward+'s. Cost, in 5
interleaved pairs: about 0.1 ms. Not a look change (it brings Mobile
back to Forward+'s sky), so not asked; it's in the live check.
Compatibility compiles it out and was unchanged in its screenshots.

`looks.gd`'s "underwater" shot wasn't under the water (the camera was at
y 15.7, above the surface); the new one puts the camera at y -2.4,
next to an island's drop-off, and `Underwater.active` showed it true.
Underwater, day, night, the night sky and storm screenshots under
Mobile and Compatibility looked right.

## Anti-aliasing under Mobile

Measured the same way (ms per view: platform out, back, island,
bridge): none 11.1, 11.7, 11.0, 16.6; MSAA 2x 14.9, 16.5, 14.7, 22.1;
4x 19.1, 20.6, 18.2, 28.0; FXAA 12.5, 13.1, 11.5, 18.9; SMAA 13.4,
14.0, 12.6, 20.3. Asked whether MSAA should now default to 2x. My
recommendation: keep both off (2x spends about half of what Mobile
saved, and the defaults must suit weak integrated GPUs), with SMAA as
the cheap way to smoother edges. **The user chose SMAA by default**
(2026-10-12); MSAA stays off. Under Compatibility, which has no
screen-space anti-aliasing, the menu shows it Off, greyed out, as
before.

## Testing

All suites pass locally (/verify). Headless tests don't render, so
they say nothing about the renderer, the dither or the anti-aliasing's
looks: the world test checks SMAA is the default (none under
Compatibility), that FXAA can be picked and kept, that a value the
setting can't take falls back to SMAA, and that Reset brings SMAA back.
The live check (`tests/live/checklist.txt`) covers the speed, the looks
by day, night, storm and under water, the lamp light, the night sky,
MSAA 2x and 4x under Mobile and the Compatibility renderer.
