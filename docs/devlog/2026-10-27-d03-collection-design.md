# D03: the collection, designed

Features: D03, D01, D02, C15, the hub

The third downtime piece designed with the user, after fishing and bug
catching (dev logs 2026-10-27-d01-fishing-design and
2026-10-27-d02-bug-catching-design). Nothing is built. The agreed
design is in NOTES → D03, and the parts that shape the hub are in
Deferred ideas → The hub.

## Offered and chosen

- **Shape:** a field guide now and the museum with the hub
  (recommended, chosen). Rejected: a museum building on the platform
  now, which would pin down part of the hub before it is designed; a
  log only, which drops the museum.
- **What happens to a catch.** I recommended recording it and
  releasing it. **The user's answer:** it disappears, "like bagged but
  without a physical item", shows up in the log and is "transferred to
  the museum automatically". Rejected: a bag of items handed in by hand
  (Animal Crossing's way), and keeping a catch on the island it came
  from (lost when the island sinks).
- **The reward.** I recommended that the hub grows as the collection
  fills. **The user went further:** the hub is a central island with
  areas designed per biome, and the museum is your catches showing up
  there. It starts empty, fills little by little, and nothing is caught
  again at the hub. So the reward is the hub filling up: no cosmetics
  or unlocks.
- **The hub's layout:** biome areas where your catches roam freely
  (the user's pick, over the fixed spot per species I recommended,
  which shows more plainly what's missing).
- **The hub's creatures follow time and weather as in the wild** (the
  user's pick over always out). That means part of the museum is
  hidden at any moment, which suits the hub as a living place.
- **No catching at the hub** (recommended, chosen). All catching
  happens out on session islands and the sea.
- **Entries: hints and records** (recommended, chosen). An uncaught
  species is a silhouette with a where-and-when hint; a caught one
  shows its first catch; fish keep their biggest size.

Then the user added: "the hub should actually be designed by me, like
as a 3d model". So its layout and size come from their model, and the
game only marks what it needs on it (spawn, bridge exits, where
creatures roam). How the model marks those is to be agreed when it
comes (NOTES → Deferred ideas → The hub).

Taken as settled without asking: the ID D03, the scope core (world
only), the collection kept between runs in `user://`, and nothing
depending on agents.
