# D01: reeling no faster than the line comes in

Features: D01

A fix for a flaky check on `main`: `world_test.gd` → `_fishing`, "reeling
drags it through the water, not up", failed about half the time (0.37,
0.04, 0.15, 0.15 m above the surface on four plain runs, limit 0.15 m;
earlier seen 0.18-0.24 m, dev log 2026-10-28-c17-opening).

## What it wasn't

- **The weather.** Its kind is random at start, but `_sky` and
  `_commands` leave it clear with `changes` off before `_fishing` runs
  (logged: clear, wind 0.10). Wind only makes the waves 1.15 times
  higher anyway. Pinning calm weather would have changed nothing.
- **Buoyancy overshooting** when a floating bobber starts being reeled
  (the 2026-10-28 guess). The nibble's dip is only 8 cm.
- **A snag.** `_lifting` stayed false the whole reel.

## What it was

A game bug, logged frame by frame. While reeling, `line_length` follows
the bobber's distance down (`minf(line_length, dist) - REEL_SPEED *
delta`), so the line is always stretched. Its pull (`stretch * 8`) was
added to the bobber's velocity every frame, with no limit, and there is
no drag in the air. The bobber went from 0.5 to 17 m/s in 30 frames
(the reel is 4 m/s), and at that speed it skipped over the crests. How
high depended on where the waves were and how stretched the line was at
the click. That broke NOTES → D01: "reel in slowly", "reeling doesn't
lift the bobber".

## The fix (the user's call, 2026-10-29)

While reeling, the bobber's velocity toward the rod's tip is capped at
`REEL_SPEED` (`Fishing._move_bobber`), lifted out of a snag too. The
pull still works as before when not reeling (a throw held at the line's
longest). Rejected: loosening the 0.15 m limit, or pinning the weather
in the test.

## Tests

The check itself, unchanged: 5 plain runs of `world_test.gd` in a row
passed, at 0.02-0.04 m above the surface. The snag check ("lifted out
at the platform's side") still passes.
