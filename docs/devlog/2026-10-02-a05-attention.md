# A05: the attention cue

Features: A05 (and W07, for the jump key left to it)

## The question

A03's orange "!" over a waiting agent is 30 cm tall, and islands are
100 m apart (clusters further), so a session waiting on a permission
was invisible from anywhere but its own island, and nothing at all
reached you while the game was in the background. With several
sessions open, that is the main way to lose time.

## Decided with the user (2026-10-02)

I offered four questions, each with a recommendation:
- **In the world:** a light beacon (chosen, recommended), a giant "!"
  over the island, or a glow on the water around it.
- **On screen:** edge pointers toward off-screen waiting islands plus a
  count (recommended), a count only (chosen), or nothing.
- **In the background:** a taskbar flash (recommended), a flash and a
  chime (chosen), or nothing.
- **A "go to whoever waited longest" key:** now (recommended) or with
  W07 (chosen).

Follow-ups: the count has no key either (my reading of "count only"
offered one; the user kept it for W07); the chime plays only when the
window isn't focused (recommended, chosen); an on/off toggle in the
sessions menu, saved (recommended, chosen) over no control or a volume
slider; and the user picks the sound (recommended): kenney.nl was
unreachable from here, and making one in code was the alternative.

## How it's built

See NOTES → A05. The beacon is a child of A03's marker, so subagents
get it for free and it follows the marker's `waiting` exactly. The
screen and window side (`Attention`) keeps its own set of waiting
agents, so "a new wait" means an agent going into `waiting`, not every
event that repeats it; that's what keeps the flash and chime to once
per wait.

## Found while building

- **The beam looked pink.** At 45–65 % opacity, orange over the blue
  sky mixes to lilac (screenshots). Now 80 % ± 15 %, and it reads
  orange under both renderers.
- **Thin at a distance:** the fog would hide it past 390 m and a fixed
  0.5 m pillar is two pixels at 300 m, so it ignores the fog and widens
  with distance from the camera (1 cm per metre).
- **The count missed sessions that arrive already waiting.** A new
  session's snapshot only emits `session_added`, not one event per
  agent; the first screenshots had beacons and no count. `Attention`
  now rescans a session on `session_added`, on a resync
  (`permissions_changed`, which also drops agents a resync no longer
  lists) and on `session_removed`. A world-test check covers it.
- The screenshot script has to wait for the real sidecar's first sync
  before adding test sessions, or the sync removes them.
- The test points the sound setting at its own file and deletes it,
  so it never touches the developer's `user://settings.cfg`.

## Checked

- World test: 14 attention checks (count by session, beacon on and
  fading out, nothing while focused, flash and chime once per wait in
  the background, a session arriving waiting, an ended one uncounted,
  the toggle, the setting kept between runs). All Godot suites pass.
- Screenshots (Forward+ and Compatibility) from another island, from
  the platform (two beacons about 200–300 m away) and close up (the "!"
  under the beam, the count in the corner).

## Not checked yet

- The taskbar flash for real (only the call is tested; headless has no
  window), and the chime, which waits for the user's file.
- macOS and Linux behaviour of `window_request_attention` (Dock bounce,
  urgent hint); only Windows is testable here.
