# W08 follow-ups: dolphin jump, shelf colours, sea floor

Features: W08, W01, W03

The user's notes after W08's live check: rising out of the water killed
your momentum ("conserve my speed a bit when leaving the water so I can
jump out"), the island's shelf went straight from its own sand colour to
the sea floor's, and the sea floor wanted more depth variation (which
noise was left to me to recommend).

## Decided (user's, 2026-10-08)

Asked as choices with a recommendation each; the user took all three
recommendations and gave no reasons beyond the choice.

- **Dolphin jump:** automatic when you come up fast, keeping all the
  speed (rejected: keeping only a share, about 70 %, for smaller hops;
  breaching only with Space held; a boost of about 1.3x, which would
  have let you land on the platform's 1 m deck and broken "out only by
  a beach").
- **Shelf colours:** a shared smooth gradient: the island's underwater
  faces use the sea floor's colour by depth, and that colour is a
  gradient instead of three bands (rejected: sharing the bands as they
  were, which keeps contour lines; a gradient on the island only, which
  can't match the bands everywhere).
- **Sea floor noise:** domain warp on the base noise, a layer of mounds,
  and spreading the base over the whole range (rejected: the same plus
  ridged sandbars, busier and at the 4 m grid's limit; mounds only,
  leaving the big shapes blobby).

## What was built

- **Breach** (`Player._swim`): at the surface, rising faster than
  `breach_speed` and not touching ground ends swimming, keeping the
  velocity. I had offered "about 3 m/s" in the question, but plain Space
  rises at 3.5 m/s (`swim_speed`), and "swimming up at the normal speed
  still floats" was part of the chosen option, so the threshold is
  4.5 m/s: only sprinting up breaches. The "not touching ground" part
  keeps sprinting up a beach the walk-out it was (the slope ride-up sets
  4.8 m/s upward, which would otherwise breach).
- **Height:** feet peak at 0.79 m above the water at the full 6 m/s,
  which is under the platform's 1 m deck. A throwaway probe sprinted at
  the platform's side from 0.05 to 2.5 m away, at four pitches, with and
  without Space: never landed on the deck (48 runs). Bridges' rails are
  2.2 m walls; their open last metre is on an island's beach.
- **Floor:** today's floor never got shallower than -18 m and three
  quarters of it sat between -22 and -30 (spread 2.7 m). Now banks reach
  -14 m, the spread is 4.5 m, and the height change per 4 m cell
  doubled (0.22 to 0.42 m). The noise costs about 1.7 µs a point, at most
  0.5 ms of a 3.85 ms chunk build (mesh and collision are the rest).
- **Colours:** `Seabed.colour` lerps sand (to -10 m), mid (-19 m) and
  deep (-28 m); `IslandGenerator._colour` returns it below -0.5 m, and
  its own `SEABED` colour is gone.

## Bugs on the way

- The colour test first compared mesh colours exactly and matched 0 of
  2962: a mesh keeps vertex colours at 8 bits a channel. It compares
  within 1/255 now.
- The breach test's first timing ended before the leap peaked; it now
  lets go of the keys once out of the water and waits until it's
  swimming again.
