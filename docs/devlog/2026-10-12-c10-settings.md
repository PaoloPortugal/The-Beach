# C10: the settings menu

Features: C10, A05, W03, W04, W09, C01, S03

The user asked for a settings menu with graphics settings after W11
(2026-10-08). Designed 2026-10-12 as choices with a recommendation each;
the user took all four and gave no reasons beyond the choice: Esc opens
it; a focused first set (graphics, mouse sensitivity and field of view,
the chime, being taken to a new island; rebinding later); tabs; changes
applied at once and saved, with Reset to defaults. NOTES.md → C10 has
the decisions and the rejected options.

## What was built, and my picks

- `Settings` (values and their file) and `SettingsMenu` (the panel),
  under `world/settings/`; `world.gd` → `apply_settings` applies them.
- Ranges and defaults: render scale 50-100 %, mouse 0.25-3x, field of
  view 60-110°, FPS caps Off/30/60/120/144/240, shadows High (as built:
  4 splits, 4096 atlas) / Low (2 splits, 2048) / Off.
- Esc: the other UIs already took Esc in `_input`, so the menu opens in
  `_unhandled_input` and only sees an Esc nobody else used. The player's
  own Esc (free the mouse) is gone: opening the menu frees it.
- The chime's toggle moved out of the sessions menu, keeping its own
  `[attention] sound` key in the same file.
- Under Compatibility, sun shadows stay off whatever the setting (Watch
  items); the menu shows Off, greyed out, rather than the saved value.
- The tabs are sized to the tallest so the panel doesn't jump (seen in
  the first screenshots).
