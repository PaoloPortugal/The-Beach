# C14: the main menu

Features: C14, C13, S07

## Where it came from

The user asked for a main menu (2026-10-26). Its shape was settled
before any code, in two rounds (NOTES C14 → Decided): over the live
world in place of S07's loading cover, a camera circling the islands
that eases down to the player on Play, Play / Settings / Credits /
Quit, and "Main menu" in the pause menu. Then, before building: the
pause menu's centred panel (not my left column), the working name as
the title (listed for the rename), Play never held back for the
sidecar (not my "wait for the sync"), and Credits as CREDITS.md itself
plus Godot's own notices.

During the build:
- The loading cover had no job left. I recommended deleting it; the
  user kept it, unused. Its tests on stand-in sidecars stay; the part
  that checked it over the world became the menu's start test.
- The user asked for "a back to main menu button in addition to the
  regular quit" in the pause menu: it was already planned (C14 →
  Decided) and went in as **Main menu** between Settings and Quit game.

## What was built

- `world/main_menu.gd` (`MainMenu`): the panel, its dim, the credits
  page, and the HUD it hides. Settings borrows `SettingsMenu.back` and
  puts the pause menu's back when Esc returns.
- `world/menu_camera.gd` (`MenuCamera`): orbit, ascend, descend. The
  circle is the middle of the platform and every island, 40 m past the
  farthest, clamped to 60-220 m so it stays inside the fog (ends at
  390 m) and the islands' draw distance. The eases blend the whole
  transform (`interpolate_with`) and the field of view with a
  smoothstep, aiming at the player's camera as it is each frame.
- `world/pause_menu.gd`: the button and `main_menu_chosen`.
- `world/world.gd`: makes both, opens the menu at the end of `_ready`
  where the cover was added, adds `main_menu.is_open()` to the
  minimap's `hidden_when`.
- `MainMenu.close_now()` for the layout and ring demos, which want the
  world at once; the bridges suite runs the layout demo, so without it
  the minimap checks there would have seen the menu.

Godot's notices: `Engine.get_license_text()` is Godot's own MIT text;
`get_copyright_info()` lists the third-party parts (FreeType, ENet,
mbedTLS and the rest) with their copyright lines, and
`get_license_info()` the licences' texts. All of it is shown, as plain
text (never read as BBCode), so a shipped build carries every notice
Godot asks for. It's built the first time Credits opens.

Links in the credits open straight in the browser: the file is ours,
not session content, so the chat's "ask before opening" (Security
rules) isn't needed. Only http(s) and mailto links are clickable
anyway (the Markdown parser).

## Not done

- No export presets exist yet. When they do, `assets/CREDITS.md` must
  be in the export's include filter (non-resource files aren't
  exported by default), or Credits says the file couldn't be read.
- The look is a placeholder, like all UI.

## Tests

`tests/world/world_test.gd`: `_menu_start` (the start, Play) and
`_main_menu` (back from the pause menu, Settings, Credits, Quit, the
circle, Play again); `_pause` checks the fourth button. All passed on
the first run.
