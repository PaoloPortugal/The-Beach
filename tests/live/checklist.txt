1. Play. Press 2: the rod
   bottom right? Settings →
   Field of view at 60 and
   110: still in place?

   the meter fills, and the
   bobber is thrown: a fuller
   charge further, aiming up
   further still?
3. The bobber floats and
   bobs on the waves? The
   line sags when slack and
   goes straight when tight?
4. Throw it onto an island,
   a bridge and the
   platform's deck: it lands
   and rolls, no message,
   and nothing ever comes?
5. Hold left click: it reels
   in slowly, rolling back
   over land; let go: it
   stops? Trees, rocks and
   steps don't get it stuck?
6. Reel part way in the
   water and let go: it
   rests, and a fish comes
   there?
7. By an island: shadows in
   the shallows? A cast near
   one brings it?
8. Nibbles visible, then the
   bite: the plunge, the
   splash ring and the "!"
   clear enough to know it's
   time to click?
9. Click on a nibble: "Too
   soon: it swam off"?
10. Click on the bite: the
   card, with New? A second
   of that species: no New?
11. Miss a bite: "It got
   away", another comes? The
   1.5 s window feels right?
12. With the line out, walk
   about: it stays out?
   Walk far (30 m): the
   bobber drags after you?
13. Cast, then each of:
   press 1, open the chat
   (E on an orchestrator),
   the map (M), jump in and
   swim: each reels in?
14. Reeling speed, the
   throw's distances and the
   bobber's bounce feel
   right?
15. Restart with
   -- --checklist --hour 22

   bobber, line, splash,
   "!", meter and card
