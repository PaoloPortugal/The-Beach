LIVE CHECK: D01 fishing
  godot --path . -- --checklist
  (add --sidecar-dev-fake if
  you like: no Claude needed)
PgDn/PgUp scroll this.

1. Play. Press 2: the rod
   shows bottom right? How
   does it look?
2. Hold left click, let go:
   a meter fills under the
   crosshair? A fuller
   charge casts further?
3. Aim at an island and at
   the platform's deck:
   no cast, "Can't cast
   onto land"?
4. The bobber rides the
   waves? The line from the
   rod's tip looks right?
5. By an island: shadows
   in the shallows? A cast
   near one brings it?
6. Nibbles visible (the
   bobber dips), then the
   bite (it goes under)?
7. Click on a nibble: "Too
   soon: it swam off"?
8. Click on the bite: the
   card, with New? A second
   of that species: no New?
9. Miss a bite: "It got
   away", and another comes?
10. Click while waiting: it
   reels in?
11. Cast, then each of:
   walk off, press 1, open
   the chat (E on an
   orchestrator), the map
   (M), jump in and swim:
   each reels in?
12. The timings, the
   distances and the bite
   window feel right?
13. Restart with
   -- --checklist --hour 22
   --weather rain: the
   bobber, line, shadows,
   meter and card readable
   at night and in rain?
