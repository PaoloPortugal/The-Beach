LIVE CHECK: D01 fishing,
the fight
  godot --path . -- --checklist
  (add --sidecar-dev-fake if
  you like: no Claude needed)
PgDn/PgUp scroll this.

1. Play. Press 2 for the
   rod. By an island, cast
   into the shallows; on the
   bite, click and keep
   holding: the fight starts
   and the fish comes in?
2. In a fight, move the
   mouse: the rod's tip
   swings, the view stays
   put? The fish drags the
   tip to its side, and
   pushing back feels like
   holding against it?
3. The rod bends more the
   harder it pulls? Near
   the limit the line goes
   straight and trembles,
   and the view shakes a
   little? Readable enough
   without a bar?
4. The shadow and the
   pulled-under bobber show
   where the fish is, with
   a splash as it turns?
   The view drifts after it
   when it swims far to a
   side?
5. Reel hard into a run
   (from the platform, the
   drop-off): "The line
   snapped"? Let go when it
   runs, and it's fine?
6. Let go while it swims at
   you: the line goes slack
   and "It slipped off the
   hook" after a couple of
   seconds?
7. Land fish of each water:
   shallows a few seconds,
   drop-off ~15 s, open sea
   (deep water, e.g. off a
   long bridge's middle)
   ~30 s? Too easy, too
   hard, too long?
8. Mid-fight press 1, open
   the map (M), or Esc: the
   fish goes, the mouse
   turns the view again?
9. With a session (not the
   fake), an agent starts
   waiting mid-fight: the
   corner count pulses once?
10. The rod's sensitivity:
   the mouse moves the tip
   too much or too little?
   (Settings → mouse
   sensitivity changes it.)
