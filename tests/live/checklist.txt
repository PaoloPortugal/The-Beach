On the platform, then on a
beach and a mountain island
(Tab: start a session on
each kind).

1. Hold Ctrl: you go down
   smoothly, walk slowly. Let
   go: up again. Height and
   speed feel right?
2. Sprint (Shift+W), press
   Ctrl: a burst, fading in
   about 1 s. Too far, too
   short, too fast?
3. Slide down a beach, or the
   mountain's meadow: keeps
   going? Up a slope: stops
   sooner?
4. Holding W, steer a slide
   with the mouse: a little,
   enough?
5. Space mid-slide: a long,
   fast jump. Land holding
   Ctrl: slides on, slowing,
   no new burst. Fair?
6. Crouch under an agent's
   desk or a low prop: you
   can't stand until out.
7. Wade into the sea crouched,
   then swim: Ctrl swims down,
   you stand on the beach.
8. My picks to confirm:
   - the burst waits 0.5 s on
     the ground after a slide
     (landing crouched slides
     on with no new burst);
   - downhill gains from about
     13 degrees;
   - 20 m/s top speed.
