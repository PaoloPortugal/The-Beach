LIVE CHECK: D01 fishing,
the fight
  godot --path . -- --checklist
  (add --sidecar-dev-fake if
  you like: no Claude needed)
PgDn/PgUp scroll this.

1. Play. Press 2 for the
   rod. By an island, cast
   into the shallows; on the
   bite, click and keep
   holding: the fight starts
   and the fish comes in?
2. In a fight, the fish
   drags the rod's tip to
   its side and the view
   turns with it? An arrow
   by the crosshair points
   the other way; push the
   mouse that way: the rod
   and view come back and
   the arrow fades?
3. Hold the rod against
   it: the fish turns and
   comes in, clearly faster
   than leaving the rod be?
4. Runs: a splash, the rod
   jerks, it pulls hard.
   Keep reeling: "The line
   snapped"? Let go: it
   takes line, stays on,
   and tires? Readable
   enough (the rod bends,
   the line trembles near
   the limit, the view
   shakes a little)?
5. Let go while it swims at
   you: the line goes slack
   and "It slipped off the
   hook" after a couple of
   seconds?
6. Walk away while reeling
   a fresh drop-off fish:
   the line strains and
   snaps? A tired small
   fish tows fine?
7. Land fish of each water:
   shallows a few seconds,
   drop-off ~15 s, open sea
   (deep water, e.g. off a
   long bridge's middle)
   ~20-35 s? Too easy, too
   hard, too long?
8. Mid-fight press 1, open
   the map (M), or Esc: the
   fish goes, the mouse
   turns the view again?
9. With a session (not the
   fake), an agent starts
   waiting mid-fight: the
   corner count pulses once?
10. The rod's sensitivity:
   the mouse moves the tip
   too much or too little?
   (Settings → mouse
   sensitivity changes it.)
