A. The start
1. Start the game. It opens
   on the main menu: "The
   Beach", Play, Settings,
   Credits, Quit, in a dark
   panel, the world dimmed
   all round? No minimap,
   crosshair or usage box?
2. The view circles slowly,
   high up, the platform and
   islands in sight? Smooth,
   no jumps as islands come
   back?
3. Tab, E, M, Esc, WASD: does
   nothing happen?
4. Click Play: the panel goes
   at once and the view
   glides down to your eyes
   on the platform (about
   2.5 s), smooth, no jump at
   the end? Then you can
   move and look, the mouse
   captured, the HUD back?

B. Back to it
5. Esc → Main menu: the view
   rises from where you look
   up onto the circle? The
   mouse free?
6. Play again: back where
   you stood?
7. Start a session (Tab, +),
   wait for its island. Esc
   → Main menu: does the
   circle widen to take the
   island in? Play: the
   session's still there?

C. Settings and Credits
8. Settings: the settings
   panel, the world still
   dimmed? Change something,
   Esc: back on the main
   menu, the change kept?
9. Play, Esc → Settings, Esc:
   back to the pause menu
   (not the main menu)?
10. Main menu → Credits: a big
   panel with the asset
   table, the shaders table,
   then "Made with Godot
   Engine" and long licence
   texts? Does it scroll
   with the wheel, and read
   well?
11. Click the Kenney link: it
   opens in your browser?
12. Esc closes the credits;
   Credits again, Back too?

D. Quit and coming back
13. With a session running:
   Quit asks first, naming
   it? Back to the game: the
   main menu still up? Quit
   again, and Quit in the
   dialog: the game closes,
   the session still running
   in its terminal?
14. Start the game again: its
   island appears standing
   behind the main menu
   within a moment? Play,
   walk to it: chat works?
15. /exit the session in its
   terminal. Esc → Main menu
   → Quit: closes without
   asking?
